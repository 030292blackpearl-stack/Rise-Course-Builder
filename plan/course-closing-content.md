## End-of-Course Case Study — "Supporting Clients with Safe Medication Reminding"

Alex has been a home service worker for the past six months in Illinois. Today, Alex is visiting Mr. Johnson, a client who needs assistance with medication reminding. Alex's role involves checking that the medication minders are appropriately labeled and marked for the correct days and times, providing verbal reminders, and ensuring Mr. Johnson's preferences and safety are respected.

• Alex (Caregiver)
• Mr. Johnson (Client)

### Decision Point 1
Alex arrives at Mr. Johnson's house. After greeting him, Alex needs to check the medication minder to ensure it is correctly labeled.

Alex: Good morning, Mr. Johnson. Let's look at your medication minder for today.
Mr. Johnson: Sure, let's take a look.

**How should Alex verify that the medication minder is correctly organized?**
A. Ensure it has clear labels for each day and time.
B. Ask Mr. Johnson to verify the labels himself.
C. Assume it's organized since it was pre-filled by a family member.
D. Only check the medication names but not the times.

Correct Answer: A
Rationale: Illinois guidelines emphasize that caregivers should personally verify that medication containers are properly labeled for both day and time.

### Decision Point 2
With the medication minder verified, Alex prepares to prompt Mr. Johnson about taking his medicine.

Alex: The labels look all set. Do you remember what time you planned to take them?
Mr. Johnson: Yes, I usually take them in the morning.

**What is an effective way for Alex to provide a verbal reminder?**
A. 'You must take your medicine now or you might forget!'
B. 'Just looking at the labels and checking them is fine.'
C. 'It's the usual time if you're ready to take your medicine.'
D. 'Do you want me to just take care of it for you?'

Correct Answer: C
Rationale: Providing a gentle reminder allows the client to maintain a sense of control and adheres to a supportive and respectful approach.

### Decision Point 3
Mr. Johnson accepts the verbal reminder and wants assistance with opening the medication container.

Mr. Johnson: Can you help me with this one? It's hard for me to open today.
Alex: Of course, let me give you a hand with that.

**What should Alex ensure when handling the medication container?**
A. Put on gloves before touching the container.
B. Handle the container carefully and open it as requested.
C. Open all containers for future use.
D. Refuse to open it and suggest Mr. Johnson asks his family.

Correct Answer: B
Rationale: Alex can assist with opening containers if requested by the client, promoting safety and dignity in handling such tasks.

## Final Assessment (10 questions)

Q1 · Objective 1 — Which action is appropriate for a Home Service Worker when assisting a client with medication reminding?
A. Select medications for the client. / B. Store medications in prescription bottles. / C. Hand the appropriately marked container to the client. / D. Change the medication schedule without consulting anyone.
**Correct: C** — Illinois guidelines allow Home Service Workers to hand appropriately marked containers to clients.

Q2 · Objective 2 — How can a Home Service Worker verify that medication containers are properly marked?
A. Ensure they are sealed with tape. / B. Verify the container is made of glass. / C. Check for clear day and time markings. / D. Confirm the label matches the client's favorite color.
**Correct: C** — Proper markings include clear day and time indications, which comply with state guidelines.

Q3 · Objective 3 — What technique should be used to remind clients about their medication schedules?
A. Provide verbal prompts at the scheduled times. / B. Leave printed schedules by the bed. / C. Send text messages every hour. / D. Write reminders on a wall.
**Correct: A** — Providing verbal prompts at scheduled times helps clients remember their medication routines.

Q4 · Objective 4 — Which statement ensures safe handling of medication containers?
A. Open all containers immediately. / B. Refill containers with any available medication. / C. Shake each container before handing it over. / D. Allow clients to open their own containers, if able.
**Correct: D** — Clients should open their own containers if they are able, preserving their independence.

Q5 · Objective 5 · True/False — It is important to report any medication taken too often to a supervisor or client advocate.
**Correct: True** — Irregularities in medication schedules should be promptly reported to ensure client safety.

Q6 · Objective 6 · True/False — Proper documentation of medication reminding activities includes noting any irregularities observed.
**Correct: True** — Documenting observed irregularities is essential for effective communication with the care team.

Q7 · Objective 7 · True/False — A Home Service Worker should make changes to a client's medication schedule if they believe it is incorrect.
**Correct: False** — Only a healthcare professional should make changes to a medication schedule, not a Home Service Worker.

Q8 · Objective 7 · Fill in the blank — The role of a Home Service Worker includes _____ the client in managing their medication schedule.
A. assisting / B. ordering / C. monitoring / D. directing
**Correct: A (assisting)** — The worker aids and supports, not directs or orders, the client's medication management.

Q9 · Objective 5 · Fill in the blank — When a medication irregularity is observed, it must be _____ immediately.
A. reported / B. documented / C. ignored / D. prevented
**Correct: A (reported)** — Reporting is crucial to allow necessary actions to be taken by authorized personnel.

Q10 · Objective 3 · Fill in the blank — Verbal prompts should be given in a way that _____ the client's understanding and comfort.
A. hinders / B. fosters / C. ignores / D. amazes
**Correct: B (fosters)** — Prompts should support and encourage the client's understanding and comfort.
